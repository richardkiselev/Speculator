Book 1: Duty

  1. Lo, the Holy Dutchman appeared.
  2. With each step he took, a tulip sprouted from the Holy Ground.
  3. The Holy Dutchman took a step with his left foot, and a tulip blossomed.
  4. The tulip was colored a beautiful golden orange. It quickly grew, and before the Dutchman took another step, the orange tulip had grown into the Sun.
  5. The Holy Dutchman took another step, this time with his right foot.
  6. From the ground, a blue tulip emerged.
  7. Before the Holy Dutchman could lift his leg, the Blue Tulip, imbued with rebelliousness, began to consume the Holy Dutchman.
  8. It consumed his foot, his knee, and his leg before the Holy Dutchman was able to move away.
  9. While the Holy Dutchman was able to create a Peg Leg for himself to use, he was unable to regenerate his old leg.
  10. In anger, the Holy Dutchman ripped out the tiny tulip.
  11. As he held the bulb in his hands, it began to grow. The Holy Dutchman realized that the Blue Tulip was full of power.
  12. The Holy Dutchman turned the Tulip's flowers into paint, and he painted a beautiful picture.
  13. From that picure came a planet: the Earth.
  14. The Earth was full of life: the Tulip's petals had imbued the painting with the Holy Dutchman's magic.
  15. From that day on, the Holy Dutchman cursed the little Earth, for it had stolen his leg.
  16. Lo, the Holy Dutchman bestowed upon the inhabitants of the earth a great stupidity.
  17. The only way to remove this stupidy from oneself would be to worship the Holy Dutchman, studying every day.
  18. By dedicating one's life to academics, one could become enlightened and reach a state of Nonamania.
  19. After reaching Nonamania, one can ascend to the Plane and become the final member of the Nine Men.
  20. When the final member of the Nine Men joins, the Holy Dutchman will be satisfied with his creation and will turn the Earth back into a picture.
